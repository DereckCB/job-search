What I am looking for, and how to use this

What I am looking for

  • Product management: hardware, IoT or manufacturing technology.
  • Technical program management in the same places. Same record, different framing.
  • Hybrid or remote, in a city with a hardware scene.
  • Where the record is strongest: owning a product end to end, suppliers, and translating between engineering and the business.

How to use this page

  • One entry per thing you actually did. Not a job, a thing: a launch, a fix, a decision, a number you moved.
  • Write the result first, with the number in it. "Cut supplier-data processing 95% with a VBA tool" beats "improved workflows".
  • Tag it with the company and the skills it proves, so a CV bullet or an interview answer is a search away.
  • Star the strongest ones. Those are the stories you tell first.
